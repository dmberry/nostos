# Provenance: Gen X cult-cinema game imagery

All images sourced from Wikimedia Commons under free licences (CC0, Public Domain, CC BY, CC BY-SA). Portraits are event/festival/red-carpet photographs of the people named; no copyrighted posters, film stills, or studio publicity photos were used, except the two genuinely public-domain *Night of the Living Dead* (1968) items. Converted to JPEG, resized to 600px wide, recompressed to 20-140KB.
