# Travel-advert strip images

Six banner thumbnails for the fake travel-advert strip. All are from Wikimedia
Commons under Public Domain, CC0, CC BY or CC BY-SA licences. All were resized to
420px wide and re-encoded as JPEG; no other alteration except where noted.

No image has an identifiable person as its subject. Where distant figures appear
they are unrecognisable at this size, and `beach-strip-01.jpg` was cropped
specifically to remove a lone distant figure at the left edge.
