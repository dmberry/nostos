# Provenance — Paranormal & Unexplained feature images

All images sourced from Wikimedia Commons under Public Domain, CC0, CC-BY, or CC-BY-SA
licences only. Each was downloaded via the Commons `Special:FilePath` endpoint, resized to
600px wide (never upscaled), and re-encoded to JPEG. Three files are narrower than 600px
because their Commons source is smaller than 600px wide and was kept at native resolution
(noted below).

The famous copyrighted / disputed paranormal images (Patterson-Gimlin frame, the Surgeon's
Photograph of "Nessie", alien-autopsy stills) were deliberately NOT used. Places, objects,
signs, and public-domain historical/vintage material stand in for them.
