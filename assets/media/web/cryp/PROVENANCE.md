## Notes

- **al-Kindi**: deliberately not sourced — no contemporaneous portrait exists (9th-century figure), as anticipated in the brief.
- Attribution obligations: the CC-BY / CC-BY-SA / CC0 items above should carry author + licence credit where the feature displays them; the CC-BY-SA works additionally require share-alike on any adapted version. Public-domain items carry no legal obligation but are credited here for record.
- Processing: originals fetched from Wikimedia Commons, resized to 600px wide with `sips` (no upscaling), re-encoded to JPEG with quality tuned to land within the 20–140KB target band. `hellman-01.jpg` (native 240px, 19.6KB) sits just under the 20KB soft floor because the Commons original is small and was not upscaled.
