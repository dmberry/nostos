## Notes on individual files

- **winograd-01.jpg** — native 500x396, kept at source width (not upscaled). This is
  the only freely licensed photograph of Terry Winograd on Commons; he is shown at a
  conference beside an anatomical model, and his name badge is visible.
- **sutherland-01.jpg** — native 420x564, kept at source width (not upscaled).
- **tx-0-01.jpg** — no free photograph of the TX-0 machine itself exists on Commons.
  This is a museum display panel captioned "MIT TX-0 Transistorized Computer Built in
  1955, Operational in 1956", showing the Philco surface-barrier transistor racks and a
  vacuum tube for comparison. Native 700x412, downsized to 600px wide.
- **tech-square-01.jpg** — no free photograph of the historic 545 Technology Square
  building (home of Project MAC and the AI Lab) exists on Commons. This is the
  present-day Technology Square development on the same Cambridge site; the buildings
  shown are modern, not the 1960s original.
- **model-railroad-01.jpg** — no freely licensed photograph of a model railroad layout
  with visible relay wiring exists on Commons. This is a generic HO-scale club layout,
  chosen for the clubroom setting.
- **papert-01.jpg** — shows Papert with the original MIT Logo turtle robot, so it
  doubles as period material for the Logo pages.
