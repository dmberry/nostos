## Notes on cultural sensitivity

For living and Indigenous traditions the selection favours museum artefacts and historical
artworks rather than sacred or ceremonial imagery used out of context: an Ife cast head
(West African cultural heritage), a British Museum Inca gold figurine, a Louvre Marquesan
ancestor figure, and a Torres Strait museum mask. Hindu material uses historical devotional
paintings (Ravi Varma; an 18th–19th c. Gita illustration) rather than contemporary worship images.

## Method

Fetched via the Wikimedia Commons API (`imageinfo` + `extmetadata` for licence/author, and
`Special:FilePath` for the source pixels), then resized to 600px wide and JPEG-recompressed with
`sips` to fall inside the 20–140KB band. Licence and author strings above were read from each
file's Commons `extmetadata` record.
