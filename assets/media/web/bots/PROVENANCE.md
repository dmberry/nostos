## Note on excluded non-free results

Twitter/X brand marks (e.g. "Twitter Logo Blue.png") on Commons are under the
**Apache License 2.0**, which is outside the kept set (CC0/PD/CC BY/CC BY-SA),
so they were not used. socialmedia-01.jpg is instead a CC BY-SA 4.0 photograph
of the Twitter logo at its NYC office.
